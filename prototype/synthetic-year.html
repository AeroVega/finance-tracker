A year of invented finances.

Upload January through December 2025 into a new empty Google Drive folder. Use the saved canonical records to test future visualizations, insights and projections.

Development only. Production financial-data persistence remains NO-GO. This page never imports your files or uploads real financial records.

What the year contains

Invented checking, savings, credit-card and brokerage accounts; recurring pay and bills; seasonal spending; paired transfers and card payments; a refund and a correction. Source observations and audit history remain inspectable.

Investment activity is preserved separately and excluded from ordinary income and spending when the engine cannot classify it. These are known subtotals, not verified complete finances. Opening balances, historical valuations and current holdings are unavailable, not zero.

Preview needs no Google authorization and writes nothing to Drive.

Choose a folder and upload

  1. Create a new empty folder in Google Drive with synthetic in its name.
  2. Check in Drive that it is empty. Limited drive.file access cannot prove that files outside this app's visibility are absent.
  3. Confirm below, then choose that folder. Existing visible files, including a prior session or database, are refused before any write.

Active app storage format: canonical version 4, with compact-json-v1 values and paged fallback. New copies use source/derived codec 2, locator catalog 3 and physical root protocol 4. Existing per-record v1 and fragmented v3 copies remain compatible. Standalone grouped v2 is refused.

Opening a copy still verifies and loads the complete ledger and retained history. This is not bounded-memory app loading. Existing folders are never silently migrated; the backup guide creates an explicit new v4 copy while preserving the old history.

Verify, recover or release

After an error, do not start another upload into the same folder. Reconnect to it, recover only if offered, then verify. An exact saved year is a no-op. Only a folder admitted as empty in this tab can retry after verified recovery to its original empty state.

After closing or reloading this tab, an empty or incomplete copy cannot be seeded again here. Preserve it for inspection and choose a new empty folder. An interrupted initial setup without a valid manifest also requires a new folder. Nothing is deleted automatically. If failed setup leaves no usable release or recovery control, close this tab, wait for its session to expire, and start in a new empty folder. Do not reopen the old folder on another device while its session may still be active.

Release before opening the same folder in another tool or device. Session coordination is best-effort, not a server lock. Navigation warnings are not guaranteed on mobile or after the browser terminates.

After verified release, open the main workspace and choose Open saved synthetic year to browse and correct the saved records. The canonical import workbench and backup and restore guide remain available for advanced checks.
