        Record reconciliation evidence

        Checking, savings and credit cards use posted cash movement. Investments and loans use statement components: enter every change that explains closing balance minus opening balance, including signs. A debt reduction is negative.                   Storage format

                  No saved copy loaded. New copies use full-snapshot v1.

                  This build stores one complete canonical ledger snapshot and a small commit envelope for each save. Use a fresh empty folder. Old-format folders are unsupported and are never silently converted.

                  Snapshots are limited to 8 MiB. Opening and recovery verify retained history; ordinary saves verify current data and the new transition, so corruption limited to historical snapshots may be discovered later. This is not bounded-memory loading. Keep this tab open during writes and use recovery after an interrupted save.
